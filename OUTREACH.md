# Outreach kit: finding and writing to clients

For Abdullah Khan / The Prism Arc. English and Roman Urdu. Everything here is a starting point: change the words until they sound like you, and **never claim a result you do not have.** Use only real projects (Alley Artificials, TheBaigArts, Car Preview, MaintainIQ) and say what they are.

Message placeholders look like `[this]`. Fill every one with something real and specific, or delete the sentence.

---

## 1. The offer in one line

- EN: I build the website and run the ads that fill your WhatsApp.
- Roman Urdu: Main aapki website banata hoon aur woh ads chalata hoon jo aapke WhatsApp ko inquiries se bhar dein.

Say it once, early. After that, talk about **their** business.

## 2. Ideal clients

Pick two groups first and do them well. A person who sells on Instagram and takes orders on WhatsApp is the closest match to your work.

| Profile | Why they fit | What to look for | What you offer first |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jewellery, gifts, florals, hampers | Same as Alley Artificials: catalogue, WhatsApp orders, product photos | Active Instagram, orders by DM, no website or a weak one | Catalogue site with WhatsApp order button, product creatives |
| Fashion and boutiques | Visual, repeat buyers, seasonal campaigns | Many posts, no checkout, link in bio goes to a chat | Lookbook site, Meta ads to WhatsApp |
| Restaurants and food | Local, phone and WhatsApp driven | Menu only as photo, no online ordering page | Menu page with order-on-WhatsApp, local ads |
| Real estate agents | Lead generation is the whole business | Listings on social only, calls from portals | Listings page, lead ads, WhatsApp follow-up |
| Car dealers and workshops | Same family as Car Preview and the quote tool | Photos shared in WhatsApp groups, no listing page | Shareable photo links, inventory page, ads |
| Artists and personal brands | Same as TheBaigArts and the creator landing page | Strong work, no home on the web | Gallery or landing page with order requests |
| Small agencies and startups | Need a reliable builder | Outdated site, slow, not mobile friendly | Rebuild, performance, maintenance |

**Not a fit (skip politely):** businesses wanting WordPress or Shopify builds (you are still learning them, so do not claim them), anyone asking for guaranteed sales, anyone with no budget for ads who wants ads.

**Markets:** Pakistan first. Then UAE (many Pakistani-run shops), UK, US, Canada, Australia. For overseas, mention time zones and that you work on WhatsApp.

## 3. Where to find leads

Do 10 good leads a day, not 100 bad ones. Keep them in a simple sheet: name, business, link, city, what is missing, date contacted, reply, next step.

### Google Maps
1. Search `[business type] in [area]`, for example `jewellery shop in Gulshan-e-Iqbal Karachi`.
2. Open businesses with reviews but **no website** or a website that is not mobile friendly.
3. Check the photos and the review text for things you can fix (no menu, wrong hours, no booking).
4. Contact through the phone or WhatsApp number on the listing. This is public business information; keep the message about the business.

### Instagram
1. Search niche hashtags and the city: `#karachijewellery`, `#lahorefashion`, and so on.
2. Good signs: 500 to 20,000 followers, posts every week, "DM to order", link in bio is empty or a chat link.
3. Note their best 3 posts and one thing to improve.
4. Follow, like two or three posts naturally, then send a DM (section 6). Do not mass-follow.

### LinkedIn
1. Search `[title] [city]`: founder, owner, marketing manager, director at small firms.
2. Prefer real estate, agencies, startups, clinics, schools, importers.
3. Read their profile and company page for one specific thing to mention.
4. Send a connection note (section 5), then the message only after they accept.

### Facebook
1. Pages and groups: local business groups, marketplace sellers, "buy and sell" groups for your city.
2. Look at pages with many posts but no website link, or ads running to a bad page (the Meta Ad Library shows which pages are advertising).
3. Message through the page inbox, or comment helpfully first. Never post offers in groups that forbid promotion.

### Directories
- Local business directories, chamber of commerce lists, exhibition and trade-fair exhibitor lists.
- Car and property portals (dealers and agents listed there).
- Delivery apps (restaurants with weak or no web presence).
- Use only the public business contact. Do not scrape personal numbers.

## 4. Qualify before you write

Spend three minutes per lead. Score each yes as 1 point.

1. Is it a real, active business (posted in the last month)?
2. Can they afford a project (not a one-person hobby page)?
3. Can I name **one specific thing** to improve?
4. Do customers already talk to them on WhatsApp or DM?
5. Is the owner or decision maker reachable?
6. Is it a niche I have real work to show for?

- **5 to 6:** write a personalized message today.
- **3 to 4:** write only if you have a very specific observation.
- **0 to 2:** skip.

Prepare one **observation** per lead: "your page has beautiful photos but the link in bio goes nowhere", "your listing shows no website", "your menu is a photo that is hard to read on a phone". If you cannot write the observation honestly, do not send.

## 5. Messages (English)

Rules for all of them: under 120 words, one idea, one easy question, no attachments in the first message, no fake urgency, no "dear sir/madam", no claim of results.

### 5.1 Cold email

**Subject options:** `Quick idea for [Business]'s website` / `[Business] on WhatsApp: one suggestion`

```
Hi [Name],

I came across [Business] on [Instagram / Google Maps] and liked [specific thing, for example the hand-made packaging in your last posts].

One thing I noticed: [specific observation, for example customers have to DM for every price and there is no page showing the catalogue].

I'm Abdullah, a web developer and digital marketer in Karachi. I build websites and run Meta and TikTok ads so that people reach a business directly on WhatsApp. I recently built a catalogue store with WhatsApp orders for a jewellery and flowers shop (Alley Artificials).

Would it help if I sent a short note on how [Business] could do the same? No cost, and no problem if the answer is no.

Abdullah Khan
The Prism Arc
[WhatsApp number] | [portfolio link]
```

### 5.2 LinkedIn

**Connection note (under 300 characters):**
```
Hi [Name], I build websites and run WhatsApp-focused ads for small businesses. I liked [specific thing about their company]. Would be good to connect.
```

**First message after they accept:**
```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I looked at [Company] and noticed [specific observation].

I help businesses get more inquiries on WhatsApp through a clear website and well-targeted ads. If improving [that area] is on your list this quarter, I can send a short, practical suggestion. If not, no worries at all.
```

### 5.3 Instagram DM

```
Assalam o Alaikum / Hi [Name]! Your [product or post] really stood out. 

Quick thought: [specific observation, one sentence]. I make websites and run ads for small shops so orders arrive on WhatsApp. Want me to send a short idea for [Business]?
```
Send nothing else until they answer. If they reply, move to WhatsApp.

### 5.4 Website-audit message (use the free audit offer)

```
Hi [Name], I reviewed [website or page] on my phone and wrote down 3 things that may be costing you inquiries: [short example, like "the order button is hard to find on mobile"].

I can send the full short audit (free, one page, in plain language). Should I send it here or on WhatsApp?
```
Only send this **after** you really did the audit. Keep the three findings true and checkable. The site's 3-6-9 hunt also offers the same free audit to visitors, so the offer is consistent.

### 5.5 Follow-up sequence (stop at any reply)

| Day | Message |
|---|---|
| 0 | First message (above) |
| 3 | "Hi [Name], just checking my note reached you. One small idea for [Business]: [one line]. Happy to share if useful." |
| 8 | "I put together a quick example of how [specific thing] could look for [Business] [link or screenshot]. Take a look when you have a minute." Send only if you really made it. |
| 15 | "I'll stop here so I don't clutter your inbox. If you ever want help with [website / ads], message me anytime. Wishing [Business] a great season." |

Do not send more than four messages. If they say no, thank them and mark the lead closed.

### 5.6 When they reply

- **"How much?"** Do not give a number blindly. Ask what they need and the rough budget, then give a range for that scope. "It depends on pages and ads. Can you tell me what you sell and where most customers come from today?"
- **"Send details."** Send one short message and your portfolio link, then ask a question.
- **"Not now."** "No problem. May I check in again in [month]?" Then diarize it.
- **"I already have someone."** "Great. If you ever want a second opinion on the site or ads, I'm glad to help."

Same rules. Written the way you would speak to a shop owner: respectful, simple, no heavy English unless it is a normal word (website, ads, WhatsApp).

### 6.5 Follow-up

| Din | Message |
|---|---|
| 0 | Pehla message |
| 3 | "Assalam o Alaikum [Name], bas check kar raha tha ke mera message mil gaya? [Business] ke liye ek chhota idea hai: [ek line]. Kaam ka laga to bata dein." |

| 15 | "Ab main aur message nahi bhejunga taake aapka inbox bhara na lage. Kabhi [website / ads] mein madad chahiye to bejhijak message karein. [Business] ke liye dua." |

Chaar se zyada message na bhejein. Agar wo na keh dein to shukriya ada karein aur lead band kar dein.

### 6.5b Jawab milne par

- **"Kitne paise?"** Andaza se rate na batayein. Pehle poochein: "Aap kya bechte hain aur zyada tar customers kahan se aate hain? Phir main aapke kaam ke hisaab se range bata dunga."
- **"Details bhej do."** Ek chhota message aur portfolio link, phir ek sawal.
- **"Abhi nahi."** "Koi baat nahi. Main [mahine] mein dobara pooch sakta hoon?"
- **"Mere paas banda hai."** "Bohat acha. Kabhi website ya ads par doosri raaye chahiye to hazir hoon."

## 7. What to show when they say yes

1. The portfolio link, on their phone first.
2. One relevant project with its **real** Live link (Alley Artificials for catalogues, TheBaigArts for artists, Car Preview for car businesses).
3. A clear scope and price in writing, in WhatsApp, before you start.
4. Do not quote ad results from other clients. Say what you will track (for example WhatsApp messages) and what you will report.

## 8. Daily and weekly routine

**Daily (60 to 90 minutes):**
- 10 new leads found and qualified (section 4).
- 5 to 8 personalized messages sent, no more.
- Replies answered within the same day.
- Follow-ups due today sent.

**Weekly:**
- Review the sheet: how many contacted, replied, call booked, won.
- Rewrite the opener that got the fewest replies.
- Add one small piece of public work (a short post about a project) so people who look you up find something fresh.

## 9. Do and do not

**Do**
- Mention something true and specific about their business.
- Ask one easy question.
- Be fine with "no".
- Use their language: Roman Urdu for most Pakistani owners, English for corporates and overseas.
- Keep the portfolio link ready and fast on mobile.

**Do not**
- Copy-paste the same message to 100 people.
- Claim sales, revenue or ad results you did not measure.
- Mention clients, numbers or awards you do not have.
- Name the auction site behind the Car Auction Quote Tool.
- Claim WordPress or Shopify skills.
- Message people who said no, or message at night. Send between about 10:00 and 18:00 in **their** time zone.
- Send bulk WhatsApp messages to people who never heard of you. It gets numbers banned and hurts your name.

## 10. After you win a client

- Confirm scope, price, timeline and what you need from them (photos, prices, logo) in one WhatsApp message.
- Take an advance as agreed, and keep payment records.
- Share a working version early, on their phone.
- With permission only, add the project to the portfolio through Admin, with the numbers they allow you to show.
